## Implementation principles

- Keep the experience useful without WebGL.
- Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Keep project links accessible through normal HTML.
- Prefer restrained depth and intentional composition over unrelated visual effects.
- Do not claim visual testing without an actual browser run.
